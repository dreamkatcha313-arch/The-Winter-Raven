A NOTE BEFORE YOU BEGIN

Knowledge, not diagnosis.

Winter Raven is an independent educational and creative project. Its articles are intended to explain psychological and related health concepts—not to diagnose, treat, or replace an assessment by a qualified professional. Experiences vary, and similar symptoms can have different causes.

Articles focus on definitions, features, context, assessment, and non-medication supports. Medication treatment is intentionally outside the scope of this archive.
