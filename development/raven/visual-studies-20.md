## Art direction findings
- Rear-view silhouette is most legible with separated primaries and a wedge tail.
- Dim side lighting can reveal feather layering without turning the bird blue.
- The storm needs separate foreground blur, mid-distance flakes and distant haze.
- Wide wingspan requires safe framing on narrow Android screens.
- Generated stills are reference concepts, not anatomical proof, physical simulation, or a reusable animated 3D model.

## Asset and production status
The 20 individual stills and a ZIP were generated as conversation artifacts. They have not been committed as binary assets to this repository and are not used on the production site. The existing procedural raven remains active. Before using stills in an animation, establish continuity of the raven anatomy and wingbeat, create a real rigged model or a controlled compositing pipeline, and verify frames at mobile size.
