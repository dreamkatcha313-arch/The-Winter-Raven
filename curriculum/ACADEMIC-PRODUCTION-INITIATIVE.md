- Claim-level source verification: record citation, relevant passage, evidence classification (established, strong, emerging, contested, historical, fictional), limitations and verification date.
- Independent scientific and pedagogical review: record reviewer, qualifications, scope, date, corrections and approval. No self-certification.
- Website: reusable two-page book, responsive single-page fallback, reduced motion, Ember Script examination, keyboard access.
- Assessment: server-authoritative grading, reviewed question bank, randomized options, authenticated attempts, regression and integration tests.
- Rewards: shared Axiom identity and persistent progression, idempotent grants, no medical data, no competing currency or test issuance.
- Release: CI, staging, live smoke checks; no verified rewards until gates pass.
