# Foundations of Neuroscience

Status: curriculum draft v0.1 (2026-11-22); educational, not diagnostic or individualized treatment advice. Review by a qualified subject-matter expert before public release.

## Learning objectives
- Identify neuron, synapse and neurotransmitter
- describe neuroplasticity without exaggeration
- explain the difference between correlation and causation.

## Lesson 1 — Core concepts
Neuroscience studies the nervous system at molecular, cellular, circuit, behavioral and systems levels. Neurons transmit electrical signals and communicate across synapses using neurotransmitters; glial cells support and modulate neural function. Brain regions operate as interconnected networks rather than isolated personality centers. Learning is associated with experience-dependent changes in neural circuits (plasticity), not a guaranteed rewiring result from any single exercise. Distinguish correlation from causation when interpreting brain imaging and behavioral studies.

## Lesson 2 — Critical thinking
Compare symptoms, duration, impairment and context. Avoid diagnosing yourself or another person from an article. Distinguish established findings from emerging hypotheses and ask whether claims are supported by clinical guidelines or peer-reviewed evidence.
