# Narcissism and Narcissistic Personality Disorder

Status: curriculum draft v0.1 (2026-11-22); educational, not diagnostic or individualized treatment advice. Review by a qualified subject-matter expert before public release.

## Learning objectives
- Distinguish ordinary narcissistic traits from NPD
- explain why a qualified assessment and impairment matter
- identify stigma-free ways to discuss behaviors.

## Lesson 1 — Core concepts
Narcissism is a spectrum of traits; confidence or occasional self-focus does not establish a personality disorder. Narcissistic personality disorder (NPD) involves a persistent, impairing pattern of grandiosity, need for admiration and reduced empathy. Clinical evaluation considers long-term patterns, context, impairment and alternatives. Causes are multifactorial; no single childhood experience determines NPD. Psychotherapy may address relationships, emotional regulation and self-esteem; do not diagnose others from online checklists.

## Lesson 2 — Critical thinking
Compare symptoms, duration, impairment and context. Avoid diagnosing yourself or another person from an article. Distinguish established findings from emerging hypotheses and ask whether claims are supported by clinical guidelines or peer-reviewed evidence.
