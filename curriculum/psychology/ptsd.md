# Post-Traumatic Stress Disorder (PTSD)

Status: curriculum draft v0.1 (2026-11-22); educational, not diagnostic or individualized treatment advice. Review by a qualified subject-matter expert before public release.

## Learning objectives
- Name the four broad symptom clusters
- distinguish a normal acute stress response from persistent PTSD
- identify why trauma-focused treatment requires appropriate support.

## Lesson 1 — Core concepts
PTSD can develop after exposure to traumatic events; most people exposed to trauma do not develop PTSD. Key symptom clusters include intrusion, avoidance, negative changes in mood/cognition and changes in arousal/reactivity. Symptoms persist and impair functioning. Evidence-based trauma-focused psychotherapies include prolonged exposure, cognitive processing therapy and EMDR; these are delivered by trained clinicians. Do not turn the curriculum into unsupervised trauma exposure exercises. Provide content notices and a skip option.

## Lesson 2 — Critical thinking
Compare symptoms, duration, impairment and context. Avoid diagnosing yourself or another person from an article. Distinguish established findings from emerging hypotheses and ask whether claims are supported by clinical guidelines or peer-reviewed evidence.
